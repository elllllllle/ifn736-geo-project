IFN736 Industry Project (Phase 2) · Group 22

The 19 test conversations

These are the multi-turn conversation scripts we run against each page variant to test whether AI assistants mention Flight Centre. Each one is grounded in Flight Centre's own Customer Persona Book and follows FC's real Dream → Plan → Engage → Book (→ Pre-trip) journey stages, replacing the original 15 single-query Phase 1 tests. See the Findings dashboard for results.

i

Turns 1 and 4 are used exactly as written; turns 2, 3 and 5 are a starting point, adapted to what the AI actually said. Turn 1 (Dream) never mentions "package," "deal," or Flight Centre by name. Turn 4 (Book) is fixed and identical across all 19 — "Who do I actually book this through?" — deliberately neutral so the closing question never hints at DIY or an agency.

Only 3 of 19 extend to a 5th (Pre-trip) turn — Q16, Q17 and Q19 — because Flight Centre's own persona data names a specific Pre-trip behaviour for those exact concerns. In-trip and Return stages are out of scope, since both happen after the booking decision is already made.

Journey stages: Dream→ Plan→ Engage→ Book→ Pre-trip* *3 of 19 only

Evolved from Phase 1 themes

Each of these 15 evolved from one of the original Phase 1 query categories — same destination/product theme, rewritten from a single search-box query into a natural multi-turn conversation.

New themes

4 additional conversations covering concerns neither the original 15 nor the earlier 23 persona conversations tested as an opening question.
