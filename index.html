What it adds up to

4.1

The same 3 conversations win, every method

Q01 (Bali couple), Q02 (family, domestic) and Q08 (family, Fiji) are the only scenarios that ever surface FC — in recall, in live search, and once content is injected. Every other persona (solo, backpacker, senior, corporate, last-minute, adventure) never produces an organic mention, method notwithstanding.

4.2

Discoverability is the bigger lever

The 5.1% → 38.1% jump only happens once FC's content is already in the conversation. Search grounding alone barely moves reach (5.1% → 23.8%, same 3 conversations) — the problem is being considered as a candidate at all, not page structure.

4.3

Structure looks promising, isn't proven yet

Excl. Book turn, full composite pages with tables (V4, Site B) lead baseline by 10-12 points — the largest gaps found — but none of the 9 variants clear statistical significance at this sample size. A lead worth re-testing, not a settled result. What does hold up regardless: the 97% named-first shift above.
