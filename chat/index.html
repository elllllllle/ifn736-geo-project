What these metrics mean

    

      Inclusion Rate and Ranking Position are checked directly against the response text — no
      judgement call involved. Relevance through Trustworthiness are scored 0–3 by Gemini judging
      its own answer: unverified self-scores, shown so you can see what the team's evaluation
      actually measures, not as a tested result.
    

    

      
Inclusion Rate

      
Whether Flight Centre is mentioned anywhere in the response at all.

      
Ranking Position

      
Which position Flight Centre is named in, relative to the other travel brands in the response — "1st" means it's named before any competitor.

      
Relevance

      
Does the response actually answer the question that was asked?

      
Response Quality

      
Is the response clear and well-structured overall, independent of whether Flight Centre appears?

      
Exposure

      
How prominently Flight Centre features when it is mentioned — a passing aside versus a genuinely prominent placement.

      
Faithful Credit

      
When Flight Centre is mentioned, is the claim about it accurate and fairly represented, not exaggerated or invented?

      
Causal Impact

      
Whether the injected page content — used only in Optimised mode — appears to have shaped this specific answer, rather than a generic response the model would give anyway.

      
Readability & Structure

      
Whether the response is easy to scan (headings, short paragraphs) rather than a wall of text.

      
Trustworthiness

      
Whether the response reads as balanced, credible advice rather than promotional copy.
