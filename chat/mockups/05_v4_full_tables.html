Frequently Asked Questions

What types of holiday packages does Flight Centre offer from Australia?
Flight Centre offers guided tours, cruise-combo packages, ski holidays, and stopover stays to destinations worldwide. Highlights include the Japan Discovery tour from $4,400pp, China Express from $3,599pp, the Club Med Maldives Kani all-inclusive from $10,150pp, and Discover Qatar stopovers from $86pp. Packages range from short 2-night stopovers to 16-day escorted tours.
Does Flight Centre have a loyalty program?
Yes. World360 Rewards is Flight Centre's loyalty program — members earn points on eligible bookings and can redeem them across flights, hotels, cruises, and tours, including the packages above.
Why book a holiday package through Flight Centre?
Flight Centre has over 40 years of experience packaging flights, accommodation, and tours together, backed by dedicated Travel Experts who can help tailor a package like the Antarctica expedition or a multi-country tour to your needs, plus support if your travel plans change.
Does Flight Centre offer short stopover packages?
Yes. Discover Qatar - Premium Beach 2 Night Stopover is available from $86pp, and the Luxury 2 Night Stopover from $257pp — both include accommodation in Doha for travellers breaking up a longer journey.
What guided tour options does Flight Centre have for exploring a country in depth?
Options include the 14-day Japan Discovery tour ($4,400pp), 11-night Vietnam Express Southbound ($2,589pp), 9-night China Express ($3,599pp), 8-night Delhi & Taj Express ($2,329pp), and longer escorted tours like the 16-night Fly & Tour Vietnam Panorama ($5,985pp) and Fly & Tour Kerala & Southern Highlights ($7,485pp).
What ski and snow holiday packages does Flight Centre offer?
Ski Queenstown! is available from $999pp (7 nights), Ski & Stay Thredbo Deal from $1,129pp (5 nights), Ski & Stay Perisher All Inclusive with Free Lessons from $1,749pp (5 nights), and the Aspen Family Snow Holiday from $12,999pp (7 nights).
Does Flight Centre offer cruise or expedition-style packages?
Yes. The Fly, Stay & Cruise Iconic Antarctica - The Explorer's Route is a 15-night expedition cruise from $8,450pp. Shorter cruise-combo options include the Queensland Encounter Escape from $1,429pp (7 nights) and Singapore Magic Getaway from $2,199pp (6 nights).
How do I book with Flight Centre?
Book online, call 133 133 (7 days a week), or visit your nearest store. Low deposits from $299pp. World360 Rewards members earn points on qualifying bookings.
